**Site search**

- A search can be narrowed by package, type, subtype, culture, continent and region; a being with homes in two regions is found under both.
- A note's name and aliases rank it first, and a result shows the note's icon or banner.
- Blog posts can be filtered by author and tag and sorted by date, and one site's search can cover every site's pages.
- A `.secret` section is kept out of the search index, as secret blocks are.
