**Content and notes**

- A PNG, WebP or SVG image's embedded provenance can state `hl:name` and `hl:primarynote`, the name and Address of the note it shows, so a portrait found on its own traces back to its being or affiliation.
- Rewriting a portrait or emblem fills both from the one being or affiliation note that names it, and the provenance check reports a stale name or an Address that names no note.
