**Scripts**

- A mapping note may state `logographic: true` for a script that writes meanings: each word is written from the lexicon, by its own writing or its parts', and a word with neither is an error rather than a sound spelling.
- Affixes and the plural and verb endings may carry their writings for such a script, and `package-build lexicon` lists each word's writing and marks the words that have none.
