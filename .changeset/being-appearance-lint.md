- `package-build lint` requires a being's `complexion` to be a list of complexion words, `[]` where no condition is established, and refuses a single word.
- `extra_features` admits only short plain descriptions of visible physical marks; colours, skin conditions, gear, voice, manner, looks, handedness, sterility, apparent age and punctuation are errors.
- `package-build npc new` writes the complexion it draws as a list.
