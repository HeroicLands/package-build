**Glyphs and cartouches**

- `[label]{.glyph slug=… frame=… size=…}` sets a sign in a line of text, and `[label]{.cartouche slug=… align=… height=… caption=…}` sets a framed seal among the words around it.
- Both appear in Foundry journals, on the website, in the editor preview and in the printed book.
- The picture is a file the package ships, `icons/<slug>.svg` for a glyph and `images/cartouches/<slug>.svg` for a cartouche, served at the same address as any other image.
