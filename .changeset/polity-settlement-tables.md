**Content and notes**

- Polities combine listed and noted settlements into direct subject tables and recursive indexes, with territorial completeness checks.
- Settlement notes require short notes; those with positive population also require a settlement type, a polity government, and a market.
- Markets use international, regional, local, or subsistence in place of numbers; populations use at most two significant digits.
- Settlement lists belong to polities; region settlement lists are no longer accepted.
- Polities with settlements, seats, or subordinate polities require a territorial population; each polity may declare only one superior.
