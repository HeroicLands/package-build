Definition lists now use a dictionary hanging indent across website, Foundry, preview and book output. Terms are bold and stay on one line; definitions share a 150px column, reduced to 100px at browser widths of 600px or less. Long terms push the first definition farther right while wrapped lines return to the shared column. Multiple definitions, rich Markdown and footnotes remain available, and infobox profiles keep their separate layout.
