- A place takes the culture of the places it sits within, and an affiliation that of the bodies it answers to; a polity with neither takes that of its seat and the places it governs.
- Content tables and book queries select on that inherited culture, and a culture note counts as its own culture.
- A note whose parents belong to different cultures is an error until it names its own.
