**Footnotes**

- A footnote referenced inside an info, warning or GM-only box, or inside a
  caption, now numbers and prints with the rest of the note instead of
  showing as literal `[^id]` text.
- A footnote definition written inside a list or a block quote, or a
  reference with no definition at the top level of the note, is now reported
  as an error instead of rendering differently — or wrongly — on each
  surface.
