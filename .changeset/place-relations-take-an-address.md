**Places** — a border's or a route's `to` names the far side by address, so a
frontier shared with a place in _another package_ is written unambiguously as
`thalorna-none-place-vylar`. A bare shortcode still names a place in this
package, which is what every border already says. Two places sharing a
shortcode in different packages no longer stand in for each other when the two
ends of a frontier are checked against each other, and a `to` naming something
that is not a place now says so.
