`content-build map --chart <shortcode>` draws the chart from a place: the map from it at a larger horizon, north up, the hops chaining on as long as none turns back, each place on the ring of its summed days and each hop beyond the neighbours drawn fainter. `--horizon <days>` sets how far the rings reach, a year by default, and a place farther than that is left out.
