**Citations**

- A dictionary cites a word from every note that marks it in italics, by its form or a variant, whatever the accents and capitals.
- A note whose name, alias, rank or office title is the word cites it too.
- A word's `attested` key is refused: delete it, and mark the word in italics where a note uses it.
