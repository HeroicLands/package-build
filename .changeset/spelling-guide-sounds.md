**Spelling guides**

- A reference note can state a setting's spelling guide as data: its letters and marks, each letter row naming the languages that write it.
- A letter row and a syllable final can give their sound in the International Phonetic Alphabet with `ipa`.
- A language's letter row can name another language by its note, as well as by one of its own tongue tags.
