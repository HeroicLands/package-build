**Figures**

- Prose refers to a figure in another note, and a reference resolves in a Foundry journal as well as on a page and in print.
- A figure drawn from a map counts as a Map, and reads the same way on every surface that numbers it.
- A picture is drawn at the size its role chooses, and never larger than it was made.
- A figure written inside a GM box, an info box or a caution stays inside it instead of becoming a page of its own.

**Dated occurrences**

- A lore note states the occasions it records, each with a date, an optional end and an optional recurrence, and the build publishes the next one that falls after the world's present. A date written in year 0 recurs on that day of every year.

**Art and addresses**

- A package may declare that an installed dependency's art answers its own addresses, and the book draws the replacement in place of what the package ships.
- A published manifest carries none of the build's own keys, and a note may still write a shared field at the position it is being retired from without the build refusing the note.
