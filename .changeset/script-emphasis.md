**Scripts**

- Emphasis written inside a script reads as that script's own mark, a red rubric or an overline, on the site, in Foundry and in the book, in a red that stays readable on every background.
- A script note states its mark in `data.script`, and a language note may state its own in `data.emphasis`; a language that marks emphasis with a word reports any written emphasis in its text.
