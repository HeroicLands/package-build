# Urban food proposals

`proposeUrbanSupply` calculates food needs for explicitly bound urban places and distributes available food over declared routes. Each city has one stable map source and one explicit identity. A burg must belong to its mapped cell; a cell placement may carry a slot. Authored households and population take precedence over map estimates, and an unknown authored value stays unknown. A mapped culture and its household size supply people per household; an explicit city household size overrides that input. A declared urban total reserves subordinate polity totals and authored cities before limiting estimated new households. Missing reservations prevent allocation against that total.

A food source states annual food surplus. Each route names a source and city, travel mode, distance in three-nautical-mile leagues, annual cargo limit and explicit access. Access must be true before food moves; neither map borders nor trade value establish a supply agreement. Source surplus is shared across cities and route cargo limits are consumed as food is sent. The settlement model applies the editable transport losses and refuses routes that lose at least half their cargo. Missing food, route or access inputs appear in `missing`; an unmet need remains visible. Local food is declared per city and is not inferred from minerals, trade or local land capacity.

Results contain estimates and preserved authored facts; no notes are written. Deterministic order follows the supplied city and route lists. Urban placement discovery, naming, row writes, type and market classification, and real-map calibration are separate work.
