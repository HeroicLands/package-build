# Rural settlement proposals

`proposeSettlements` accepts normalized Azgaar cells, explicit source identities and note bindings, declared land rules, culture household sizes, existing facts, subordinate polity totals and a rural population. It returns proposals and the unchanged existing facts; it does not name settlements or write notes. It reserves existing rural households before distributing the remaining people across declared placement capacities. Existing household counts take precedence over population figures and map estimates. An explicitly unknown household count prevents distribution until its contribution is known.

A declared rural total includes the supplied subordinate rural totals. Each subordinate polity names its cells and its rural total once; its settlements are excluded from the direct allocation. When there are no subordinate polities and no declared rural total, the selected map cells' rural population estimates supply the total. Urban target selection and urban food supply are separate inputs to the wider authoring operation.

Each placement names its map cell and a stable cell or burg identity. Several cell settlements use distinct slots. A placement states its acreage and the cell's total acres; their sum cannot exceed that total. Existing identities or explicit note bindings preserve their authored facts and reserve their acreage. Missing or conflicting identities, culture sizes, acreage or land rules stop the proposal. One matching declared land rule supplies a profile, with optional conditions for biome, elevation, water and route group; a placement can override that profile. These rules are supplied data, not an inferred standard. A burg uses its own explicitly mapped culture. The map does not establish holders, descriptions, named notes or discovered minerals.

Distribution and food calculations use the [settlement model](settlement-model.md). Names, detailed route-based urban draw, row writing, type and market classification, and the real-map calibration are outside this calculation.
