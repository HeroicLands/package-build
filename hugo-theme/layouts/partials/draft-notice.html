"Draft" names the writing, so it reads as rough prose; what has to land is that the facts may change. The label and the sentence are one unit, and the sentence carries the meaning by itself — which is the floor this is built to, because the mark is a third-party font and the rule is a stylesheet, and either can be absent. The sentence sets in italic and the label in bold roman. Every other word on the page is the world describing itself; this sentence is the page speaking about its own state, and italic is how a reader is told the voice has changed.
