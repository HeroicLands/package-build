This partial and the three under `infobox/` switch on a section's `layout` and a value's `kind` and on nothing else — never on a note type, never on a field name. That is what lets a field added to a type appear here with no edit. A box is a `

It is what lets several boxes stack for nothing — a note box and one box per system are three summary lines when closed, rather than three panels burying the prose. Two kinds of box, and they answer emptiness differently: - A **note box** with no section holding anything is not drawn. A heading over nothing asserts a fact that is not there. - A **system box** carries a `statement` where it has nothing to show, and the statement is drawn in place of the sections. Which silence this is — the system compiled no document, or it compiled one holding nothing the profile has not already said — is decided by the build, because a reader meeting one page cannot tell the two apart and neither can a template. A system that does not map the note's type at all sends no box, and nothing is drawn.
